
\chapter{Generator Parameter Details}

\label{app:generator-parameters}
This appendix records the main configurable quantities used by the
Graph-Conditioned Diffusion Generator. The purpose is
reproducibility rather than method definition: Chapter~\ref{ch:generator}
describes the modelling choices, while the tables below identify the
parameters that instantiate those choices in experiments. Long code-level
names are avoided in the tables so that the appendix remains readable; the
corresponding experiment configuration files record the exact implementation
identifiers.

\section{Representation and Conditioning}

\centering
\footnotesize
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{0.34\textwidth}X@{}}
\toprule
Parameter / symbol & Role \\
\midrule
Maximum number of node rows, \(N_{\max}\) & Size of the padded node feature matrix. Graphs with fewer than \(N_{\max}\) nodes are padded with non-existent node rows. \\
Node-label vocabulary size, \(K_V=|\mathcal{T}_V|\) & Number of categorical node-label classes learned from the training set. \\
Node structural feature dimension, \(d_r\) & Dimension of the node-level structural representation \(r_i\). \\
Node feature dimension, \(d_{\mathrm{feat}}=2+K_V+d_r\) & Width of each row \(x_i=[m_i,g_i,q_i,r_i]\) in the node feature matrix. \\
Node feature scaling & Scales node-feature coordinates before denoising. The scaler is fitted on real node rows from the training graphs, after optional node-feature dimensionality reduction, and is then kept fixed. Padding rows are excluded from fitting, and the existence component is kept as a binary mask. \\
Random row permutation & Optional training-time augmentation that permutes the rows of the padded node feature matrix, so the denoiser is not tied to one arbitrary node ordering. \\
Matched-row loss & Optional clean-matrix alignment loss. A Hungarian assignment matches predicted rows to target rows using selected continuous structural coordinates, and the reconstruction loss is evaluated after this matching. This affects training only; it is not a sampling-time decoder. \\
Graph-level condition, \(C\in\mathbb{R}^{d_C}\) & Continuous graph-level vector used to condition generation. In the thesis experiments this is usually produced by a graph-kernel encoder and may be reduced in dimension. \\
Condition scaling & Standardises graph-level conditions before they are supplied to the denoiser. The scaler is fitted on the training conditions and then kept fixed for validation, held-out conditions, and generated graphs. \\
Condition dimensionality reduction & Optional fixed transformation applied to high-dimensional graph conditions. In the reported experiments this is typically truncated \gls{svd}: the projection is fitted once on the training conditions and then reused unchanged. \\
Number of retained condition components & Output dimension of the reduced condition vector, for example 128. \\
Prefix dimensions kept unreduced & Number of leading condition dimensions kept outside the dimensionality-reduction transform when some condition entries have direct semantic meaning. \\

\section{QM9 Molecular Generation Experiment Settings}
\label{app:qm9-molecular-settings}

The molecular experiments compare the proposed graph-conditioned diffusion
generator with a conditional DiGress-style molecular generator under the same
graph-level conditioning protocol. Both models use the same condition
construction. First, an \gls{nsppk} feature vector is computed for each
molecular graph. Because this feature vector is high-dimensional, a truncated
\gls{svd} projection is fitted on the training graphs and used to map the
feature vector to \(128\) dimensions. The fitted projection, together with the
training-set mean and standard deviation used for standardisation, is then kept
fixed and reused unchanged for held-out condition graphs, generated graphs, and
both generators. In this sense, ``frozen'' means fitted once on the training
set and not refitted during evaluation. The condition vector is therefore a
continuous graph-level representation rather than a molecule class label. The
workflow also computes auxiliary molecular diagnostics for checking validity,
uniqueness, novelty, and copying, but the main chapter reports the RDKit
visualisations and scalar-property \gls{mmd} comparisons.
